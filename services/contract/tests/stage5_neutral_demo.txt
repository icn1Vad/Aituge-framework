Party A may terminate this agreement immediately for convenience without compensation, while Party B may terminate only after Party A has failed to pay for ninety days.

Party B shall keep confidential information secret for five years and may disclose it only to personnel who need access. All intellectual property created under this agreement belongs exclusively to Party A after payment.
