# Report Risks and Actions Skill

Use this auxiliary skill for the Risks, Open Questions, and Next Actions section
of a report.

Cover:

- Risks that could make the report wrong or incomplete.
- Open questions that need follow-up.
- Concrete next actions with owners or sequence when possible.

Do not end with vague continuation language. The section should make the report
feel complete even when more research remains.
