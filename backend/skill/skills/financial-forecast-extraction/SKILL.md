# Financial Forecast Extraction

Extract only the financial fields listed in the current item. Preserve forecast data as year-keyed rows. Do not assume that `t0` means the investment year or the first forecast year; retain explicit calendar years until that business definition is confirmed. Keep revenue, net profit, operating cost, total profit, IRR, NPV, payback period, and ROI distinct. Preserve units and negative values. Follow the shared Smart Fill, citation, and structured-output skills.
